  NOTE ON REPRODUCIBILITY
----------------------------------------------------------------

  Every dataset is generated from a fixed seed, so the entire study
  regenerates identically. Every measurement is written to disk as
  it is taken, which is why two power cuts and three MariaDB crashes
  during collection cost no data.
